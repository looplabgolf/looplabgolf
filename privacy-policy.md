# Privacy Policy for Looplab

**Last updated: 06.05.2026

## Overview

Looplab is a personal golf tracking app that runs entirely on your 
device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal 
data to any server.

## What data Looplab uses

All data you enter into Looplab is stored **locally on your device 
only**. This includes:

- Your name and profile picture
- Your handicap index and round history
- Course information you add
- Score data, statistics, and notes

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it 
yourself.

## What we don't do

- We don't use analytics tools
- We don't track your usage
- We don't show ads
- We don't share data with third parties
- We don't use cookies
- We have no servers, no accounts, no logins


## Data export and deletion

You can export all your data as JSON or CSV at any time via Settings 
→ Privacy & Data → Export Data.

You can delete all your data at any time via Settings → Privacy & 
Data → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app.

## Children's privacy

Looplab is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly 
collect data from children.

## Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. 
Material changes will be announced in the app.
